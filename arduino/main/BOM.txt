==========================================================================
 DECK SIFT  -  BILL OF MATERIALS  (v1 card sorter)
==========================================================================
 One hopper-fed, Arduino/ESP32-driven card sorter that routes cards into
 seven bins. Print this page and tick items off as you buy them.

 Print the parts first: 3d model/card_sorter.3mf (slicer-ready mesh) or
 card_sorter_decksift.3mf (DeckSift's revised parts). The Fusion 360
 source is 3d model/Card Sorter.f3d.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 CONTROLLER (pick ONE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 [ ]  1   Arduino Uno R4 Minima (ABX0080)      - original reference board
 [ ]  1   ESP32-S3 DevKit (CH340/CH343 or      - DeckSift's primary; native
           native-USB type)                      USB or bridge - see
                                                 arduino/main/CONTROLLERS.md
                                                 for logic-level wiring

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 ELECTRONICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 [ ]  1   PCA9685 16-channel 12-bit PWM/servo driver (I2C, all 10 servos)
 [ ]  9   SG90 (or MG90S) micro servo, positional 180 deg
          (3 per module: bottom trapdoor, paddle gate, pusher)
 [ ]  1   SG90 servo, modified for CONTINUOUS rotation (the feeder)
 [ ]  1   White 5 mm LED + 100-220 ohm resistor (scan light, optional but
          recommended for holo detection) - PCA9685 ch0 / firmware LED 1
 [ ]  3   Status LEDs + resistors (optional): green (ch1), red (ch2),
          orange (ch3) - operating / fault / comms lamps

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 SENSING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 [ ]  4   Reflective/obstacle IR sensor modules, 3-pin
          (VCC, GND, digital OUT) - module gates 1-3 + hopper throat
          NOTE on 3.3 V boards (ESP32-S3/Pico/STM32): use 3.3 V modules
          or a voltage divider - their GPIOs are NOT 5 V tolerant

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 POWER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 [ ]  1   5 V regulated power supply, 4-10 A (the servo bus - do NOT run
          10 servos off the board's USB 5 V)
 [ ]  1   DC barrel jack or screw-terminal pigtail for the PSU
 [ ]  1   USB cable for the controller (USB-A-to-USB-C for the R4; the
          ESP32-S3 dev kit's own cable)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 STRUCTURAL / PRINTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 [ ]  -   PLA or PETG filament (per your slicer's estimate)
 [ ]  6   G20 o-ring (feeder roller grip)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 FASTENERS & WIRING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 [ ] 22   M3 x 6 screws (14 = bin bases to housing; 8 = base panels to base)
 [ ]  8   M3 nuts (base panel screws)
 [ ]  2   M3 x 8 screws (hopper tube)
 [ ] 33   M2 x 4 screws, 11 per module (flappers, pusher arms, IR mounts)
 [ ]  8   M2 x 6 screws (Arduino + PCA9685 to base panels)
 [ ] 10   servo horn screws (included with the servos)
 [ ]  1   roll 22-26 AWG hookup wire (IR sensors)
 [ ] ~50  Dupont connectors (only if adding connectors to IR wires)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 TOOLS (already have?)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 [ ]     Small Philips screwdriver set
 [ ]     Soldering iron + solder (IR sensor wires, LED + resistor joints)
 [ ]     Wire strippers / flush cutters
 [ ]     Zip ties / cable management (optional)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 * Common ground is MANDATORY: PSU GND -> PCA9685 GND -> controller GND.
 * The PCA9685 logic VCC is 5 V on the Uno R4, 3.3 V on ESP32-S3/Pico/STM32.
 * Servo signals stay 5 V via the PCA9685's V+ rail on every board.
 * Bulk capacitance (1000-2200 uF) across the servo rail is cheap insurance.
 * Full wiring + per-board details: arduino/main/BUILD.md, CONTROLLERS.md
 * The scan light wiring: LED anode -> PCA9685 ch0 pin (via resistor),
   cathode -> GND (the PCA9685 sinks current).
